# Bearing Inspection Procedure

Inspect bearings when remaining useful life is low, vibration-related sensor trends increase, or previous bearing wear is recorded. The technician should check lubrication, surface wear, abnormal heat, vibration signatures, and alignment. If risk is high or critical, schedule inspection before continued heavy operation.

Recommended action sequence:

1. Review latest sensor trend and RUL prediction.
2. Stop operation when the machine is in a critical risk range.
3. Inspect bearing lubrication and visible wear.
4. Verify alignment and vibration readings.
5. Replace the bearing if wear exceeds tolerance.
6. Record findings in maintenance history.
