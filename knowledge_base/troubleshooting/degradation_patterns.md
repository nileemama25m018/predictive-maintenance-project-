# Sensor Degradation Patterns

Gradual monotonic movement in temperature, pressure, vibration, or flow sensors can indicate degradation. Sudden spikes should be checked against operating settings and known transient operating conditions. A maintenance recommendation should combine model prediction, recent sensor patterns, maintenance history, and documented failure modes.

Short RUL with increasing anomaly score should be treated as higher priority than short RUL alone. Maintenance teams should verify the physical component before replacement.
